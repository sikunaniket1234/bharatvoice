How speech translation works

Four steps, usually a few seconds end to end.

  1. ListenYour speech is turned into text.
  2. Clean upMixed or noisy phrases are tidied.
  3. TranslateThe sentence moves to your target language.
  4. SpeakThe translation is read aloud.
